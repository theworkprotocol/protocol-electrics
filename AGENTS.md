# Agent bookability

`agent-bookability-spec.md` (repo root) is the standing spec for making this site findable/bookable by AI agents — phased work, hard requirements (no CAPTCHA, no login, no payment walls on booking paths, honest structured data). Consult it before touching enquiry/booking flows, robots, sitemap, or structured data. Prices shown anywhere must come from Monique's price book, never be hardcoded.
